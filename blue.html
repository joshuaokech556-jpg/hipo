Around Thika

Thika is known for its waterfalls, pineapples and the road that links it to Nairobi.

  • Chania Falls

    Short drive from town. A good stop for a picnic.

  • Fourteen Falls

    A wide waterfall on the Athi River, about 30 minutes away.

  • Ol Donyo Sabuk

    A hill and national park with buffalo, views and hiking trails.

  • Pineapple farms

    Fields around Thika grow much of Kenya's pineapple.
